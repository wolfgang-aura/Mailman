## What dgunning's commit fixed

His comment splits the 18 failures into six real losses and twelve
whitespace-only moves.

- **Plain-text filings.** `.txt` primary documents reach the parser raw, or
  wrapped in `<html><body><div>`. Their line breaks are the structure, so 8-K
  item headers and a 2001 20-F's Item 7 were lost. He marked both inputs
  `white-space: pre-wrap`, which our `_is_preformatted` already honoured.
- **Block children of an inline wrapper.** In `<font><p>..</p>\n<p>..</p></font>`
  the newline is the only separator between paragraphs. Our collapse joined a
  2005 CORRESP letter into one line. He kept that break.
- **424B cover agents.** Once a wrapped line joins correctly,
  `_resolve_abbreviation`'s capture opened on the previous sentence's tail.
  He trimmed it.
- The other twelve were whitespace-only: `dt1f1` length pins moved by one or
  two characters, and four text baselines were re-captured with his
  whitespace-only checker.
